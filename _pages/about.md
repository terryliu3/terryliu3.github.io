I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at [KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/), advised by Prof. Francesco Orabona.

My research interests are **online learning**, **uncertainty quantification**, and **optimization**. Much of my recent work is on online conformal prediction and sequential testing: building methods with provable coverage and false-alarm guarantees that need no hyperparameter tuning, using tools from online learning such as regret reductions and universal portfolio algorithms. I also work on the convergence theory of stochastic optimization methods.

Before my PhD, I received an MS in Applied Mathematics from KAUST, where I worked with Prof. David Ketcheson on explicit Runge–Kutta methods for quadratic optimization. Before that, I earned a BS in Geophysics from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where my research focused on seismic tomography.

Outside of research, I enjoy Olympic weightlifting, powerlifting, drumming, and cooking.
