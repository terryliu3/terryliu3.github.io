I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at [King Abdullah University of Science and Technology](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/)(KAUST), advised by [Prof. Francesco Orabona](https://francesco.orabona.com).

My research interests are **online learning theory**, **uncertainty quantification**, and **optimization**. Much of my recent work is on conformal prediction under (arbitrary) distribution under shifts and sequential testing. Algorithmically speaking, I focus on building methods with provable guarantees that need no hyperparameter tuning. With such a goal in mind, the methodology relies heavily on tools from coin betting and portfolio algorithms. I also work on the analysis of stochastic optimization methods. Of course, I inherit a (large) ambient background from my advisor:), and have a natural interest in theoretical aspects of online learning (bandits, min-max problems, you name it).

Before my PhD, I received an MS in Applied Mathematics from KAUST, where I worked with Prof. David Ketcheson on explicit Runge–Kutta methods for quadratic optimization (although a bit niche, I really like the theory I built). Before that, I earned a BS in Geophysics from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where my research focused on seismic tomography (inverse problems).

Outside of research, I enjoy Olympic weightlifting, powerlifting, drumming, and cooking.
